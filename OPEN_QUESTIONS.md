# Open questions

These questions must be answered through interviews, prototypes, and real
contracts. Architecture should not silently answer them by accident.

Answers and source evidence for the first candidate are tracked in the
[first-pilot discovery packet](docs/discovery/FIRST_PILOT_PACKET.md). A field is
not answered merely because the current shutdown-dose hypothesis suggests a
plausible value.

## Must answer before the first pilot

1. Which specific user, benchmark, or organization supplies the first painful
   bounded question?
2. What decision follows from its answer, and what does delay cost today?
3. What exact evidence must an independent verifier reproduce?
4. Who or what record owns each method and its applicability boundary, and
   what validation evidence supports that boundary?
5. Which solver licenses permit automated invocation and redistribution of
   adapters, manifests, and derived evidence?
6. What data may leave the customer environment, if any?
7. What is the authoritative unit and uncertainty representation between each
   proposed capability?
8. What invalidates a prior result after an input, method, dataset, policy, or
   review changes?
9. Which preflight and applicability facts are provider assertions, runner
   measurements, validated input facts, or independently reproduced records?
10. Which policy combinations have a formally decidable “tightens” relation and
    which require a new authorized policy record?

## Must answer before commercial beta

1. Is the buyer paying for a contract, a private control plane, provider access,
   or some combination?
2. How is a contract priced before its computational difficulty is known?
3. How are capability providers paid without creating incentives for favorable
   verdicts or excessive compute?
4. Who bears responsibility for a defective adapter, invalid method,
   misconfigured input, or incorrect policy?
5. What qualification framework is testable and credible for the first market
   without implying regulatory approval or requiring a universal professional
   sign-off?
6. Which parts of the runtime, schemas, verifier, and SDK must be open to make
   portability independently credible?
7. Can multiple providers implement the same capability type without semantic
   drift?
8. What security, identity, signing, export-control, and retention controls are
   required?
9. What semantic profiles must remain replayable for the intended evidence
   retention period, and how are historical kernels distributed safely?

## Must answer before 1.0

1. What narrow claim can Core 1.0 honestly make, and for which supported domain?
2. Can an independent implementation verify an exported evidence package?
3. Can Core detect every modeled invalidation path and rerun only affected work?
4. Can two organizations reproduce a campaign within its declared numerical and
   environmental tolerances?
5. Does a second independently validated provider create a substitutable result or merely a
   different method?
6. Does contract-level pricing remain profitable while Core minimizes compute?
7. What does long-term archival verification require when software, hardware,
   data, and licenses disappear?

## Naming and legal

- Trademark clearance for “Avila Core” has not been documented.
- The source, schema, SDK, and brand licenses have not been selected.
- Terms governing scientific claims, provider responsibility, evidence
  ownership, and limitation of liability do not exist yet.
- No outside patent assessment exists for the semantic compiler, admission
  kernel, signed reuse, or selective-recomputation mechanisms. That fact does
  not gate private development or technical verdicts.
- The date and exact content of every public disclosure relevant to a possible
  invention must be preserved. New enabling technical disclosures should not
  be published until Avila has consciously chosen patent, defensive
  publication, trade-secret, or open-standard treatment.
