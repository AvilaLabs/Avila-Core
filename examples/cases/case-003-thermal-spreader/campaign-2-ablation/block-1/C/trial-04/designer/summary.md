| candidate | design | rationale |

| c-0000 | 10 mm graphite | Reproduces the prior Core-scored 10mm graphite plate exactly (areal mass 18 kg/m2, thickness 10mm). That prior result showed screen=403K (margin 17K to the 420K THERM-R1 limit), FE hotspot=325K (margin 15K to the 340K THERM-R2 limit), mass=18 kg/m2 (margin 82kg to the 100kg/m2 THERM-R3 limit), thickness=10mm (margin 30mm to the 40mm THERM-R4 limit) -- all four requirements pass with comfortable margin. Included as a verified safety anchor: it is the lightest all-pass design in the prior record, so even if every thinner bet below fails THERM-R2, this candidate is expected to still pass and match (not beat) the prior best mass. |
| c-0001 | 9 mm graphite | 9mm graphite, areal mass 16.2 kg/m2 (10% lighter than the prior 10mm-graphite record). The prior data show FE hotspot temperature is essentially flat (322-325K) across 10-30mm for a given material, i.e. plate thickness in that range barely affects THERM-R2, and the pessimistic THERM-R1 screen falls (gets safer) as thickness drops, since it scales with thickness/conductivity. A 1mm reduction from the proven 10mm point is a small, low-risk step off the verified plateau, so I expect this to pass all four requirements while being lighter than the prior record. |
| c-0002 | 8 mm graphite | 8mm graphite, areal mass 14.4 kg/m2. Continuing to step down from the verified 10mm graphite point, still well inside the thickness range (10-30mm) where THERM-R2 was flat for every material in the prior sweep, and THERM-R1 only improves as thickness drops. Mass and thickness margins remain huge (THERM-R3 limit 100 kg/m2, THERM-R4 limit 40mm), so the only open question is whether the plate is still thick enough for adequate lateral spreading; 8mm is a modest, conservative step below the tested floor. |
| c-0003 | 7 mm graphite | 7mm graphite, areal mass 12.6 kg/m2 (30% lighter than the prior best). Graphite has the best conductivity-to-density ratio of the seven listed materials for this purpose (150 W/mK at only 1800 kg/m3, versus e.g. aluminium at 200 W/mK / 2700 kg/m3 or aluminium_nitride at 170 W/mK / 3260 kg/m3), so among single-material plates it should always be the lightest choice for a given thermal performance level. This point extends the thickness sweep further below the verified 10-30mm plateau. |
| c-0004 | 6 mm graphite | 6mm graphite, areal mass 10.8 kg/m2 (40% lighter than the prior 18 kg/m2 record). This is my central, best-supported guess for a genuinely lighter yet still-passing plate: it is close enough to the verified 10mm floor that I expect the still-large FE hotspot margin (15K at 10mm) to absorb any modest increase in spreading resistance from the reduced thickness, while THERM-R1/R3/R4 remain trivially satisfied. |
| c-0005 | 5 mm graphite | 5mm graphite, areal mass 9.0 kg/m2 -- exactly half the mass of the prior best all-pass design. THERM-R1 screen estimate at this thickness (per the linear trend fit to the prior data, T~=399+60*L_mm/k) is only ~401K, far under 420K, and THERM-R3/R4 are not remotely close to their limits. The open risk is entirely on THERM-R2 (FE hotspot, 340K limit): the prior sweep never tested below 10mm, so I cannot confirm the flat 322-325K plateau continues here. This is my primary candidate for 'lightest design I believe passes,' balancing a real mass improvement against the unverified region below 10mm. |
| c-0006 | 4 mm graphite | 4mm graphite, areal mass 7.2 kg/m2. Included as a speculative but physically plausible lighter alternative in case the spreading plateau extends this far. |
| c-0007 | 3 mm graphite | 3mm graphite, areal mass 5.4 kg/m2. Confidence is lower than the 5-6mm candidates given the total absence of data this far below the tested floor. |
| c-0008 | 2 mm graphite | 2mm graphite, areal mass 3.6 kg/m2. Retained in the set specifically because the brief allows up to 12 candidates and a genuinely light passing design, if it exists, is more likely to be found by sampling across a wide thickness range than by clustering near the known-safe point. |
| c-0009 | 1 mm graphite | 1mm graphite, areal mass 1.8 kg/m2. The lightest possible single-layer graphite candidate in this set, included as the extreme end of the thickness sweep. Included for completeness of the sweep, not as my primary recommendation. |
| c-0010 | 1 mm copper + 5 mm graphite | 1mm copper (facing the heated side) over 5mm graphite, areal mass 17.96 kg/m2, total thickness 6mm. Here the cap sits on a lighter graphite core instead of aluminium. Total mass is close to the prior 10mm-graphite record but at 6mm instead of 10mm thickness, giving a different, hedged bet: if a bare 5-6mm graphite plate alone is marginal on THERM-R2, this copper-faced version should have a better hotspot margin at similar mass. |
| c-0011 | 1 mm copper + 3 mm graphite | 1mm copper (facing the heated side) over 3mm graphite, areal mass 14.36 kg/m2, total thickness 4mm -- 20% lighter than the prior 18 kg/m2 record. Combines the near-source spreading benefit of a thin copper face (as seen boosting hotspot performance in the prior copper+aluminium constellation) with a thin, light graphite bulk layer. This is my second-choice 'lightest believed passing' candidate: it trades a small mass premium (versus bare 3-4mm graphite) for a plausibly safer THERM-R2 margin, since the copper face targets the requirement most likely to bind at low thickness. |
