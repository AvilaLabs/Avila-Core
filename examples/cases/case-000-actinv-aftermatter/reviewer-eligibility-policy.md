# CASE-000 reviewer eligibility policy

- **Policy ID:** `avila-labs.internal/case-000-reviewer-eligibility`
- **Revision:** 1
- **Status:** internal specimen policy; no reviewer has been authenticated or
  accepted under it

An approving reviewer for CASE-000 must:

- currently perform or independently review low-level radioactive-waste
  classification or disposition work;
- be professionally competent to assess the bounded 10 CFR 61.55
  implementation, the stated facility-screen limitations, and the
  applicability of the frozen activated-metal specimen;
- be a different person from the requester, method owner, capability provider,
  and executor; and
- review the exact hash-bound ACTINV inventory and Aftermatter route-result
  artifacts presented by the campaign, including their qualification blocks
  and missing facts.

Approval under this policy is a human governance decision. It does not turn
ACTINV, Aftermatter, or Avila Core into qualified regulatory software and does
not establish facility acceptance, packaging, transportation, shipment, or
operational authorization.
